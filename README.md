The Multiplayer Leaderboard Mod adds an in-game leaderboard to Beat Saber multiplayer. It lets you keep track of how everyone is performing without needing to check scores separately.

## Features

- **Player Names:** Shows the names of players in the lobby.
- **Rankings:** Displays each player's current position on the leaderboard.
- **Live Scores:** Shows each player's score and updates as they play.
- **Accuracy:** Displays each player's accuracy percentage.
- **World-Space Display:** The leaderboard stays in a fixed position in the game world instead of following your headset when you look around.
- **Custom Positioning:** Move the leaderboard to a position that works best for you.
- **Appearance Settings:** Adjust the overall size, text size, spacing, and background dimensions.
- **Saved Settings:** Your positioning and appearance preferences are saved so you don't have to set them up again every time you launch the game.
- **Test Mode:** Allows you to display the leaderboard outside multiplayer for positioning and appearance adjustments.

## How It Works

Once you're in a multiplayer session, the leaderboard displays the players along with their names, scores, accuracy percentages, and current rankings.

As players earn points throughout the song, the leaderboard updates to reflect their performance. Players are arranged according to their scores, making it easy to see who's currently in the lead.

The leaderboard uses the available gameplay and multiplayer information from Beat Saber to display player statistics.

## Customization

The mod includes its own settings menu under the Mods category in Beat Saber.

From there, you can:
- Turn the leaderboard on or off.
- Enable Test Mode to adjust it outside multiplayer.
- Change its position in the game world.
- Adjust its overall scale.
- Change text sizes and row spacing.
- Adjust the background width and height.
- Save your settings or restore the defaults.

You can also use Beat Saber's desktop laser pointer to click and drag the leaderboard into a different position.

## Installation

1. Make sure you have a compatible Beat Saber mod loader installed.
2. Place `MultiplayerLeaderboard.dll` into your Beat Saber `Plugins` folder.
3. Launch Beat Saber.
4. Open the Mods category and look for **Multiplayer Leaderboard**.

Enjoy keeping track of everyone's performance during multiplayer sessions!
