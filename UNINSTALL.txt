MULTIPLAYER LEADERBOARD - UNINSTALLATION
========================================

1. Close Beat Saber completely.
2. Open your Beat Saber folder, then the "Plugins" folder.
3. Delete this one file:
      MultiplayerLeaderboard.dll
4. (Optional) Delete the log folder:
      ...\Beat Saber\Logs\MultiplayerLeaderboard

That is all. The mod writes nothing else, changes no game files, and leaves no
settings behind. Your other mods are not affected.
